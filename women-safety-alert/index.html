    Safety Tips & Emergency Numbers

    🔒 Safety Tips

    • Stay aware of your surroundings and avoid distractions like headphones in unfamiliar areas.
    • Share your travel plans and expected arrival time with a trusted person before you leave.
    • Walk in well-lit, populated areas and avoid shortcuts through isolated routes.
    • Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, move toward a public place or enter a shop.
    • Keep your phone charged and have emergency contacts saved and easily accessible.
    • If followed, change direction, enter a public building, or call for help loudly.
    • Use this app's SOS button immediately if you feel threatened — don't wait.
